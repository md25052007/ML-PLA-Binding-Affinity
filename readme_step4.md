# Step 4 Execution: Running Final Evaluation on Kaggle

Ah, I understand now! The `mlpla.ipynb` training notebook was executed on your friend's Kaggle account, and you manually downloaded just the `ckpt_ep340.pt` file to your local machine. Because your local Windows machine is missing the C++ `libdgl.dll` dependencies required by the Deep Graph Library (DGL) on Python 3.13, we must run the inference on Kaggle.

However, since the training was done on someone else's machine, there is a very important technical hurdle we must manage. 

## 🛑 The Missing Link: `vae_model.ckpt`
The main Dual-Track Interaction (DTI) model (`ckpt_ep340.pt`) relies entirely on the Protein representations generated by the **Pre-trained VAE Model**. 

Because `ckpt_ep340.pt` was trained using a specific VAE model from your friend's run, **you absolutely must have their exact `vae_model.ckpt` to do the final evaluation.** If you use an "old" VAE model from your `downloads_from_kaggle` directory, the protein embeddings will be complete gibberish to the epoch 340 model, and your metrics will be terrible!

## 🚀 How to Execute your Final Evaluation on Kaggle

Here are your paths forward depending on what access you have.

### Option A: Import your Friend's Notebook Directly (Easiest!)
If your friend makes their `mlpla` notebook public (or shares it with you):
1. Create a **New Notebook** in your Kaggle.
2. Click **Add Data** -> Select the **Notebook Output** tab.
3. Search for your friend's `mlpla` notebook and add it. 
4. Copy the code from `kaggle_predict_only_notebook.py` into your notebook.
5. In the script, change `INPUT_BASE` to match the path of your friend's imported notebook (e.g., `"/kaggle/input/mlpla"`).
6. Upload your `ckpt_ep340.pt` as a dataset and point `CKPT_340_PATH` to it.
7. Click **Run All**. It will gracefully take the VAE and graphs from your friend's run, and evaluate using your Epoch 340 weights.

### Option B: Ask your Friend for the VAE File
If you cannot link the notebook directly:
1. Ask your friend to send you the `vae_model.ckpt` file from their `model_save/.../VAE/` directory.
2. Ask for the `config.yaml` from that run.
3. Upload `ckpt_ep340.pt`, `vae_model.ckpt`, and `config.yaml` as a Kaggle dataset.
4. Modify `kaggle_predict_only_notebook.py` to point directly to these files.
